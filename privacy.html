Privacy policy

celera-rclone is used only by its owner, to upload files from their own computer to their own Google Drive.

  • It accesses Google Drive only on the owner's computer, with the owner's own sign-in.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and it sends no data to anyone else.
  • The Google sign-in token stays on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: github.com/vinner3.
